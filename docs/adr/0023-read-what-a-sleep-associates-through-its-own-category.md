# Read what a sleep associates through its own category

Samsung Health offers one associated read in v1.1.0, and it belongs to sleep: given the UID of a night, it returns the blood oxygen and skin temperature measured during it. Unlike the route of an exercise (ADR 0019), those are not values inside the parent record. Each is a Health Record of its own category, with its own UID, and the time range read and the changes feed of that category return it too. The sleep capability therefore declares no `ASSOCIATED` read, and its envelope carries only what exists inside the night: its sessions and their stages, which have no identity of their own and travel in the sleep envelope the way the route travels in the exercise one.

Nesting the associated points in the sleep would give each of them two Health Record Identities, or one identity reached by two paths. A night edited at the source would be observed again carrying copies of points whose own category had already delivered them, and a point removed at the source would survive in the last version of every night that ever carried it. What the associated read adds over the categories' own reads is only which night a point belongs to, and the period of the night already says that: a point belongs to the sleep whose period contains it, which a query can join on.

The consequence is that the blood oxygen and skin temperature of a night reach the read model only once their own categories are synchronized, and only if their own read permissions are granted. Until then a night is complete without them, because they were never part of it.

The stages keep the constant Samsung Health names them with, and no mapper declares them as an enum: nothing in this repository decides anything by the stage, so a constant a later SDK adds is carried to the read model rather than reported as a gap. The API checks only that a stage names something and says when it began and ended, because the read model casts both times.
